Moderation

Tools for your staff

10 commands
/banNeeds Ban Members

Ban a member for good or for a set time, or softban them to wipe their messages.

  • member: The member to ban.
  • reason optional: Why. Saved in the audit log and the case history, and sent to them.
/channelNeeds Manage Channels

Lock, unlock, hide or slow down a channel.

  • channel optional: The channel to change. Leave empty to use this channel.
/clearNeeds Manage Messages

Bulk-delete messages in a channel, filtered by age, amount and who sent them.

  • channel optional: The channel to clean up. Leave empty to use this channel.
/jailNeeds Manage Roles

Lock a member in a jail channel without their roles, then free them with roles back.

  • member optional: Who to jail or set free. Leave empty to pick from a list.
Jail: 5 settings on the dashboard

Takes a member's roles away and keeps them in one jail channel until staff release them, then gives everything back.

Settings: Jail role, Jail channel, Give back the roles they had before, Roles to give when released, Reflection message

/kickNeeds Kick Members

Remove a member from the server. They can come back with a new invite.

  • member: The member to kick.
  • reason optional: Why. Saved in the audit log and the case history, and sent to them.
/lockdownNeeds Manage Channels

Lock or unlock every channel at once, and end a raid lockdown.

Server Lockdown: 2 settings on the dashboard

While it's on, only staff can type in any channel, for raids or emergencies. Switching it off puts everything back exactly as it was. Staff can also use /lockdown in Discord.

Settings: Channels to leave open, Message to post

/timeoutNeeds Timeout Members

Mute a member for a set time so they can't chat, talk or react.

  • member: The member to time out (or to remove a timeout from).
  • reason optional: Why. Saved in the audit log and the case history, and sent to them.
/unbanNeeds Ban Members

Browse the ban list, see why people were banned and unban them.

/warnNeeds Timeout Members

Give a member an official warning that's saved to their case history.

  • member: The member to warn.
  • reason optional: Why. Saved in the audit log and the case history, and sent to them.
/warningsNeeds Timeout Members

Look up a member's warnings, kicks, bans and timeouts, add staff notes or clear warnings.

  • member optional: Whose history to show. Leave empty to pick someone.
Warnings: 3 settings on the dashboard

Admins give official warnings with /warn. BEV keeps a case history for every member and can punish automatically when warnings add up.

Settings: DM members their warning, Warnings expire after, Automatic punishments
