📨 Invite Tracker

Shows who invited each new member, keeps an invites leaderboard and can give roles for inviting people.

Also known as: invite tracking who invited invite leaderboard invite logs

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Join log channel (a channel)
    Posts who invited each new member
  • Show how many people the inviter has invited (on/off)
    The join log says how many people the inviter has brought in so far, like: cbevs has invited 20 people
  • Invite rewards (a list)
    Each one has (up to 10):
    • Invites needed
    • Role to give

Commands

  • /invites: See how many people you've invited and the server's invite leaderboard.
