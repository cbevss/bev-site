🧯 Anti-Spam

Catches message floods, copy-pasted spam, mass pings, walls of capitals, emoji spam and glitchy text. Also reports ghost pings.

Also known as: anti-spam spam filter mention spam flood protection caps filter

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Messages allowed in the window (a choice)
    Choices: 4 messages, 5 messages, 6 messages, 8 messages, 10 messages
  • Time window (a choice)
    Choices: 3 seconds, 5 seconds, 8 seconds, 10 seconds
  • Same message repeated (a choice)
    Choices: Allow repeats, 3 times in a row, 4 times in a row, 5 times in a row
  • Mentions in one message (a choice)
    Choices: No limit, 4 mentions, 6 mentions, 8 mentions, 10 mentions
  • Capital letters (a choice)
    Messages longer than 15 letters
    Choices: No limit, 70% capitals, 80% capitals, 90% capitals
  • Emojis in one message (a choice)
    Choices: No limit, 10 emojis, 15 emojis, 20 emojis
  • Block glitchy (zalgo) text (on/off)
  • Punishment (a choice)
    Choices: Delete only, Delete and warn, Delete and time out
  • Timeout length (a choice)
    Choices: 1 minute, 5 minutes, 10 minutes, 1 hour
  • Report ghost pings (on/off)
    Reposts who pinged someone and quickly deleted it
  • Channels to ignore (channels)
