🪜 Punishment Ladder

Repeat offenders go up a ladder instead of getting each filter's own punishment: first a timeout, then jail, then a ban. BEV remembers each member's step, and it starts again after a month without an offence.

Also known as: punishment ladder auto punish escalating punishments warn thresholds

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Offences that count (several choices)
    Ticked filters use the ladder instead of their own punishment. Their message is still deleted
    Choices: Spam (Anti-Spam), Blocked words (Word Filter), Links (Anti-Link Filter), Scam links (Scam Link Blocker), Typing in the honeypot trap
  • 1st offence: time out for (a choice)
    Choices: 10 minutes, 1 hour, 6 hours, 1 day, 1 week
  • 2nd offence: jail for (a choice)
    Uses the Jail feature, so switch Jail on too
    Choices: Until staff free them, 6 hours, 1 day, 3 days, 1 week
  • Start again after (a choice)
    Choices: 7 days without an offence, 14 days without an offence, 30 days without an offence, 60 days without an offence, 90 days without an offence
