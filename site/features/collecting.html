🃏 Character Collecting

Members roll for anime and game characters with /collect → Roll and race to claim them: each character can only belong to one member here. Build a collection and give characters away.

Also known as: collectibles cards gacha collection

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Rolls per hour (a choice)
    Choices: 5, 10, 15, 25
  • Wait between claims (a choice)
    Choices: 1 hour, 3 hours, 6 hours, 12 hours

Commands

  • /collect: Roll for anime and game characters and race to claim them.
    • Roll button: Roll for a random character. Anyone can press Claim on one nobody has yet.
    • Collection button: See your collection, or someone else's.
    • Give button: Give one of your characters to another member.
