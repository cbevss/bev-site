⭐ Leveling & XP

Members earn XP for chatting and talking in voice. Add level-up rewards, XP boosts for roles, double XP events and a weekly top chatter role.

Also known as: levels XP rank card leaderboard level roles level up rewards MEE6 levels

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Level-up messages (a choice)
    Choices: In the channel they levelled up in, In one channel, In a DM, Don't announce
  • Level-up channel (a channel)
  • Chat XP cooldown (a choice)
    Choices: Every message, 15 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute, 2 minutes
  • Daily XP limit (a choice)
    Chat and voice XP stop for the day once someone has earned this much (boosts included). It resets at midnight UTC. Changes made with the dashboard's member tools don't count.
    Choices: 2,000 XP a day (Max Level takes over a year), 3,000 XP a day (Max Level takes 8+ months), 4,000 XP a day (Max Level takes 6+ months), 6,000 XP a day (Max Level takes 4+ months), 10,000 XP a day (Max Level takes 2+ months), No limit
  • Voice XP (on/off)
  • Voice XP only while unmuted (on/off)
    Muted or deafened people don't earn voice XP, and it takes at least 2 unmuted people in the channel.
  • XP multiplier (a choice)
    Choices: Normal XP, Double XP, Triple XP
  • Rank card style (a choice)
    Choices: Picture card, Text card
  • Level rewards (a list)
    A role for any level from 1 to 100 (Max Level), up to one for every level. Each one can be changed or removed at any time.
    Each one has (up to 100):
    • Level
    • Role
  • Keep lower reward roles (on/off)
  • Unlock GIF perms at a level (on/off)
    GIF perms are Embed Links and Attach Files: GIFs, pictures and link previews. When this is on, BEV takes them away from every other role, and members get them with the GIF role when they reach the level. Off: everyone keeps them, unless you remove them yourself.
  • Level that unlocks GIF perms (a number)
  • GIF perms role (a choice)
    Choices: Let BEV create a GIF Perms role, Use a role I pick
  • Role that gives GIF perms (a role)
    BEV gives this role Embed Links and Attach Files, and gives it to members when they reach the level
  • Roles that keep GIF perms (roles)
    Like your staff roles. Admins and bots always keep them
  • XP boosts for roles (a list)
    Each one has (up to 25):
    • Role
    • Extra XP (+10%, +25%, +50%, +75%, +100%)
  • Weekly top chatter role (a role)
  • Show leaderboard on the website (on/off)

Commands

  • /leaderboard: See the top members in this server, ranked by level or by coins.
  • /rank: Show a member's level, XP progress and place on the server leaderboard.
