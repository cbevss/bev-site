🐾 Pets & Battles

Members catch pets of different rarities with /pet → Catch, pick a team of 3, and battle each other's teams for coins. Pets level up as they battle.

Also known as: pets pet battles virtual pet

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Wait between catches (a choice)
    Choices: 10 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours
  • Biggest bet on a battle (a number)
    0 means battles are just for fun

Commands

  • /pet: Catch pets, build a team of 3 and battle other members.
    • Catch button: Go looking for a new pet. Rarer pets are stronger.
    • List button: See your pets (or someone else's) and your battle team.
    • Team button: Pick up to 3 pets for battles (by their numbers from /pet → List).
    • Rename button: Give one of your pets a name.
    • Release button: Let one of your pets go.
    • Battle button: Challenge another member's pets to a battle, with an optional bet.
