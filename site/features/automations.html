⚙️ Automations

Build your own rules without code: when someone joins, reaches a level, says a word, gets a role or boosts, BEV can give or take a role, give coins, post a message or DM them, only if they pass a check you choose. Each run is logged.

Also known as: automation workflows triggers and actions if this then that auto roles on level rule builder no-code automations

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Rules (a list)
    Each one has (up to 50):
    • Name (only admins see it)
    • When (Someone joins the server, Someone reaches a level, Someone says a word or phrase, Someone gets a role, Someone boosts the server)
    • Level
    • Word or phrase
    • Role
    • Only if (No check, They have a role, They don't have a role, Their account is at least so many days old, They're at least a level)
    • Role to check
    • Days or level
    • Then (Give them a role, Take a role away, Give them coins, Post a message in a channel, Send them a DM)
    • Role
    • Coins
    • Channel
    • Message
