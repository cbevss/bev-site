👋 Welcome, Goodbye & Boosts

Greets new members with a message and a picture card, can DM them your rules, says goodbye when people leave and thanks boosters. Use {user}, {name}, {server} and {count}.

Also known as: welcome message welcome card welcome image goodbye message leave message boost message join message

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Welcome channel (a channel)
  • Category for the channel BEV makes (a channel)
    If BEV makes this feature's channel (when none is picked), it goes in this category. Leave it empty for the top of the server.
  • Welcome message (text)
  • Say who invited them (on/off)
    Adds who invited them under the welcome message. Needs the Invite Tracker switched on. You can also put {inviter} anywhere in the message.
  • Say how many people their inviter has invited (on/off)
    Like: cbevs has invited 20 people. You can also put {invites} in the message.
  • Picture welcome card (on/off)
  • Card colour (a choice)
    Choices: Blue, Purple, Green, Red, Gold, Pink
  • Welcome DM (on/off)
  • Welcome DM message (text)
  • Goodbye channel (a channel)
  • Goodbye message (text)
  • Boost thank-you channel (a channel)
  • Boost thank-you message (text)
