🎂 Birthdays

Members save their birthday with /my-birthday. On the day, BEV posts a celebration and gives a birthday role for 24 hours.

Also known as: birthday birthday role birthday announcements

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Announcement channel (a channel)
  • Birthday role (a role)

Commands

  • /my-birthday: Save or remove your birthday so the server can celebrate it with you.
