🔴 Live Stream Alerts

Checks your streamers every 3 minutes and posts one alert when they go live on Twitch, YouTube, Kick or TikTok, with their profile picture and a live preview that keeps refreshing while they're live. Give someone's accounts the same creator name and they get one alert when they're live on several platforms at once, and each account can have its own message and roles to ping.

Also known as: Twitch notifications YouTube live alerts TikTok live Kick alerts go live notifications stream notifications multi-platform creators

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Streamers (a list)
    Each one has (up to 25):
    • Platform (Twitch, YouTube, Kick, TikTok)
    • Username
    • Alert channel
    • Creator name (optional)
    • Message above the alert (optional)
    • Roles to ping for this account (optional)
  • Who to ping (a choice)
    Choices: Roles (pick them in Roles to ping), @here (only people who are online), @everyone, Nobody
  • Roles to ping (roles)
    Used when Who to ping is set to roles. Leave it empty to ping the role called Stream Alerts, if your server has one.
