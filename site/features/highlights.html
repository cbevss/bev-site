🔔 Highlights

Members choose words, and BEV DMs them when someone says one in a channel they can see, with the message and a link. Nobody is told while they're already chatting in that channel. Members use /highlight to set their words.

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Words per member (a number)
  • Channels to skip (channels)

Commands

  • /highlight: Get a DM when someone says your words.
    • /highlight add: Add a word or phrase to your highlights.
    • /highlight remove: Remove a word from your highlights.
    • /highlight list: See your highlight words.
    • /highlight pause: Stop highlight DMs for a while, or start them again.
