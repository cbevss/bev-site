🛡️ Anti-Nuke Protection

Watches the audit log for anyone who bans, kicks, prunes members or deletes roles or channels too fast, stops them straight away, and can rebuild what they deleted. It also watches new webhooks and bots, and stops webhook spam.

Also known as: anti-nuke server protection mass ban protection rogue admin protection audit log watcher

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Actions allowed per minute (a choice)
    The next destructive action after this gets them stopped
    Choices: 1 per minute, 2 per minute, 3 per minute, 5 per minute, 10 per minute
  • Punishment (a choice)
    What happens to someone who goes over the limit
    Choices: Remove all their roles, Kick them, Ban them
  • Rebuild deleted channels and roles (on/off)
    Recreates what they deleted in that minute
  • DM the owner when it triggers (on/off)
    Sends the server owner an alert
  • Webhooks made by people who aren't trusted (a choice)
    Trusted: the server owner and anyone on the Whitelist
    Choices: Do nothing, Tell the server log, Delete them straight away
  • Stop webhook spam (on/off)
    A webhook that pings @everyone or posts more than 5 messages in 10 seconds is deleted. BEV's own webhooks are never touched
  • Bots added by people who aren't trusted (a choice)
    Dangerous: Administrator, Manage Server, Roles, Channels or Webhooks, Ban, Kick, Mention Everyone and Timeout
    Choices: Do nothing, Tell the server log, Take away their dangerous permissions
  • Trusted bots (members)
    These bots are never checked
  • Trusted roles (roles)
    Anti-Nuke never stops people with these roles. Only pick roles you fully trust
  • Trusted people (members)
    Anti-Nuke never stops these people
  • Alert when a role gets dangerous permissions (on/off)
    Says which powerful permissions were added (like Administrator or Ban Members), to which role and by whom
  • Alert when important server settings are weakened (on/off)
    Verification level lowered, content filter turned down, vanity invite changed, or 2FA for moderators turned off
