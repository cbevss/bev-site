🐉 Boss Battles

A boss appears and the whole server fights it together with one Attack button. If it falls in time, everyone who hit it shares the coins by how much damage they did. Summon one on the dashboard, or let one turn up on its own.

Also known as: boss battle raid boss boss fight world boss server event co-op battle

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Channel bosses appear in (a channel)
  • Bosses turn up on their own (a choice)
    Choices: Never (only when summoned), Every 6 hours, Every 12 hours, Once a day, Every 3 days, Once a week
  • Time to beat it (a choice)
    Choices: 10 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours
  • Coins shared out when it falls (a number)
  • Role to ping when one appears (a role)
