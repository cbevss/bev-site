🎵 Music Player

Play songs in voice with /music play: search SoundCloud by name or paste a SoundCloud or direct audio link. There's a queue, skip, pause, loop, shuffle and volume. YouTube and Spotify can't be played (their rules forbid bots playing them).

Also known as: music bot play music SoundCloud music queue DJ

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • DJ role (a role)
    Optional. If set, only admins and this role can skip, stop or change other people's songs
  • Longest queue (a number)
  • Longest song (minutes) (a number)

Commands

  • /music: Play YouTube, Spotify or SoundCloud songs in voice, with a queue.
    • /music skip: Skip the song that's playing.
    • /music stop: Stop the music, clear the queue and leave the voice channel.
    • /music pause: Pause or carry on with the song that's playing.
    • /music queue: See what's playing and what's next.
    • /music loop: Repeat the song that's playing, the whole queue, or nothing.
    • /music volume: Change the music volume, from 1 to 100.
    • /music shuffle: Shuffle the songs waiting in the queue.
    • /music remove: Take a song out of the queue by its number.
    • /music play: Play a song, a song by an artist, or a mix of an artist's songs. Links work too.
    • /music playlist add: Add the song that's playing, or a song you search for, to one of your playlists.
    • /music playlist show: See the songs in one of your playlists, or all your playlists.
    • /music playlist play: Play one of your playlists.
    • /music playlist remove: Take a song out of one of your playlists, by its number.
    • /music playlist delete: Delete one of your playlists.
