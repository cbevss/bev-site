Commands

  • /bet: Casino games: blackjack, slots, roulette, dice, higher-or-lower, crash and the lottery.
    • Blackjack button: Play blackjack against the dealer: get closer to 21 than them without going over.

    • Roulette button: Bet on a colour, odd or even, a dozen or one number, then spin the wheel.
    • Dice button: Bet on two dice landing over 7, under 7, exactly 7 or doubles.
    • Higher lower button: Guess if the next card is higher or lower to grow your pot, and cash out any time.
    • Crash button: Watch the multiplier climb and cash out before the rocket crashes.
    • Lottery button: Buy tickets for this server's weekly lottery. One ticket wins the whole pot.
  • /coinflip: Bet coins on heads or tails. Win and you double your bet, lose and it's gone.
