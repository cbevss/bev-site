🎈 Fun Commands

8-ball, would-you-rather, truth or dare, memes, roleplay actions with anime GIFs (hug, pat, kiss, slap and more) with /fun, and picture edits of anyone's avatar (Wanted poster, jail bars, RIP and more) with /image.

Also known as: fun commands memes 8ball jokes hug slap

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Commands

  • /fun: Play with the magic 8-ball, would you rather, truth or dare, hugs and high-fives.
    • 8ball button: Ask the magic 8-ball a yes-or-no question and get its answer.
    • Would you rather button: Post a would-you-rather question that everyone can vote on.
    • Truth or dare button: Get a truth or a dare. Press the buttons to keep the game going.
    • Hug button: Give another member a hug. They can hug you back.
    • High five button: Give another member a high-five. They can high-five you back.
    • Pat button: Pat another member, with an anime GIF.
    • Kiss button: Kiss another member, with an anime GIF.
    • Slap button: Slap another member, with an anime GIF.
    • Cuddle button: Cuddle another member, with an anime GIF.
    • Poke button: Poke another member, with an anime GIF.
    • Bite button: Bite another member, with an anime GIF.
    • Tickle button: Tickle another member, with an anime GIF.
    • Feed button: Feed another member, with an anime GIF.
    • Handhold button: Handhold another member, with an anime GIF.
    • Punch button: Punch another member, with an anime GIF.
    • Wave button: Wave, on your own or with someone, with an anime GIF.
    • Cry button: Cry, on your own or with someone, with an anime GIF.
    • Dance button: Dance, on your own or with someone, with an anime GIF.
    • Laugh button: Laugh, on your own or with someone, with an anime GIF.
    • Meme button: A random meme from Reddit (safe for work only), with a button for another.
  • /image: Fun picture edits of someone's avatar: Wanted poster, jail bars, RIP and more.
    • Wanted button: A Wanted poster with a coin reward, made from someone's avatar.
    • Jail button: Behind bars, made from someone's avatar.
    • Rip button: A gravestone, made from someone's avatar.
    • Wasted button: Grey, with WASTED across it, made from someone's avatar.
    • Pixel button: Turned into pixel art, made from someone's avatar.
