🎟️ Raffles

Post a raffle people join with a button, now or at a time you schedule. Make it free, cost coins, or need a level, and limit it to roles. When time's up BEV picks a winner for each prize you list, announces what everyone won, and winners see their prize privately.

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Channel to post in (a channel)
  • Title (text)
  • Message (text)
  • When to post it (a choice)
    Choices: As soon as I press Start, At a date and time I pick
  • Post it on (text)
    Pick the day, then type the time (24-hour) and pick the time zone. The raffle runs for the time below from then.
  • Time zone (a choice)
    Choices: Abidjan (Côte d’Ivoire) · UTC+0, Accra (Ghana) · UTC+0, Adak (United States) · UTC-10, Addis Ababa (Ethiopia) · UTC+3, Adelaide (Australia) · UTC+10:30 … (418 in all)
  • Runs for (a number)
  • Minutes, hours or days (a choice)
    Choices: Minutes, Hours, Days
  • How people enter (a choice)
    Choices: Free for everyone, Pay coins to enter, Need a minimum level, Need a level and pay coins
  • Coins to enter (a number)
  • Minimum level (a number)
  • Only these roles can enter (roles)
    Leave empty to let everyone enter.
  • Button text (text)
  • Post a message when someone enters (a choice)
    A message every time someone enters a raffle: who, which raffle, the coins they paid and how many have entered.
    Choices: In Server Logs, In Moderation Logs, Don't post
  • How winners get their prize (a choice)
    With DMs, anyone whose DMs are closed can still use the Claim button on the announcement.
    Choices: A Claim button: only the winner sees their prize, BEV DMs it to each winner
  • Prizes (one winner each) (a list)
    Add one prize per winner. The title shows in the announcement; the prize message is only shown to that winner.
    Each one has (up to 20):
    • Prize title
    • What the winner sees (private)
