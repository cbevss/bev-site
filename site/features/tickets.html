🎫 Support Tickets

Posts a Create Support Ticket button. Each press opens a private channel for that member and your staff, with close, reopen, transcript and delete buttons.

Also known as: ticket system support tickets help desk ticket panel transcripts

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Category for ticket channels (a channel)
  • Channel with the ticket button (a channel)
  • Staff role that sees tickets (a role)
  • Saved replies (a list)
    Pick one in the ticket inbox to fill in the reply. {user} is the person's name, {server} the server's
    Each one has (up to 25):
    • Name
    • Reply
  • Escalate unanswered tickets after (a choice)
    BEV raises the priority and posts a reminder in the ticket when nobody from the staff has answered
    Choices: Never, 1 hour, 4 hours, 12 hours, 1 day, 2 days
  • Ping this role when escalating (a role)
    Optional
  • Close quiet tickets after (a choice)
    When nobody has written in a ticket for this long, BEV warns in it. If still nobody writes, it closes a while later
    Choices: Never, 1 day, 2 days, 3 days, 1 week
