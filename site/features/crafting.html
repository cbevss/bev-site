⚒️ Crafting

Members turn items into better ones with /craft, like iron and stone into an Iron Sword. Comes with 12 recipes that use what /explore finds, and you can add your own. Crafted items can be sold, gifted, traded and collected.

Also known as: craft crafting system recipes make items forge

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Ready-made recipes (on/off)
    The 12 recipes that use /explore finds
  • Your own recipes (a list)
    Each one has (up to 40):
    • What it makes
    • How many it makes
    • What it takes
    • Coins it costs (0 for none)

Commands

  • /craft: Make better items out of the ones you have.
