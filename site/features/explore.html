🎣 Fishing, Hunting & Mining

Members go fishing, hunting or mining with /explore to collect items of different rarities and sell them for coins.

Also known as: fishing hunting mining exploring RPG

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Wait between trips (a choice)
    Choices: 5 minutes, 10 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour

Commands

  • /explore: Go fishing, hunting or mining for items you can sell for coins.
