🌦️ Weather & Time

/weather for any place, and /time to see the local time of any member who has set their time zone.

Also known as: weather time zones world clock

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Commands

  • /time: Set your time zone, or see what time it is for another member.
  • /weather: Get the current weather and forecast for any place.
