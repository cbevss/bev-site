⚠️ Warnings

Admins give official warnings with /warn. BEV keeps a case history for every member and can punish automatically when warnings add up.

Also known as: warn warn system infractions strikes case history

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • DM members their warning (on/off)
  • Warnings expire after (a choice)
    Choices: Never, 30 days, 90 days, 180 days
  • Automatic punishments (a list)
    Each one has (up to 10):
    • Number of warnings
    • Punishment (Time out, Kick, Ban)
    • Timeout minutes

Commands

  • /warnings: Look up a member's warnings, kicks, bans and timeouts, add staff notes or clear warnings.
