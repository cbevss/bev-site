🪪 Profiles, Marriage & Rep

/profile shows a member's card: bio, level, coins, rep points, badges and who they're married to. Members set a bio with /bio, propose with /marry (the other person has to accept) and give someone a rep point with /rep.

Also known as: profile marriage marry reputation rep bio card

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Giving rep (a choice)
    Choices: Once every 12 hours, Once a day, Once a week
  • Allow marriage (on/off)

Commands

  • /bio: Set the short bio shown on your profile card.
  • /divorce: End your marriage.
  • /marry: Propose to another member. They have to accept.
  • /profile: Show your profile card, or someone else's: bio, level, coins, rep, badges and partner.
  • /rep: Give another member a reputation point.
