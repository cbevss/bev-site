💰 Economy & Shop

Your own currency (coins, or whatever you call it) for chatting, /daily rewards, /work, /pay, a bank, robbing and a shop selling roles and items.

Also known as: economy coins currency shop daily reward work command bank

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Pause the shop (on/off)
  • Currency name (text)
    What BEV calls your money, like gems or credits
  • Currency name for one (text)
    As in “1 gem”
  • Currency icon (text)
    An emoji, or one of your server's emojis written like <:gem:123456789012345678>
  • Starting coins (a number)
  • Daily reward (a number)
  • Weekly reward (a number)
  • Work pay (up to) (a number)
  • Allow robbing (on/off)
  • Shop items (a list)
    Each one has (up to 60):
    • Item name
    • Type (Gives a role, Double XP boost, Lottery ticket, Collectible)
    • Role (for role items)
    • How long they keep the role (Forever, 1 hour, 6 hours, 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 30 days)
    • When they get it (Straight away when they buy it, It goes in their inventory to /use later)
    • How long the boost lasts (1 hour, 6 hours, 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 30 days)
    • Price
    • Description

Commands

  • /balance: Check a member's wallet and bank, see the richest members and open the shop.
  • /bank: Move coins between your wallet and the bank, where they can't be stolen.
  • /daily: Claim your daily and weekly coin rewards and keep your streak going.
  • /inventory: See everything in your inventory and how many of each you have.
  • /pay: Send some of your coins to another member.
  • /rob: Try to steal coins from another member's wallet. Get caught and you pay a fine.
  • /shop: Browse the shop, buy roles and items, and see or use your inventory.
  • /use: Use a role or boost from your inventory.
  • /work: Work a shift for coins. Better jobs unlock as you level up.
