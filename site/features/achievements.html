🏅 Achievements & Badges

Over 70 badges for chatting, levels, coins, work, games, exploring, rep, quests, music, the shop, voice and loyalty. Switch any of them off, add your own badges that give a role, and see everyone's progress with /achievements.

Also known as: achievements badges milestones

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.
