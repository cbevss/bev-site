🏅 Achievements & Badges

Members unlock badges for milestones like their first message, level 10, 10,000 coins or getting married. Badges show on /profile, /achievements shows everyone's progress, and you can add your own badges that give a role.

Also known as: achievements badges milestones

A free BEV dashboard feature

Switch it on and set it up on the BEV dashboard. Everything below can be changed there, with a preview before anything is posted.

Settings

  • Announce new badges (on/off)
  • Announce them in (a channel)
    Optional. Empty: where they earned it
  • Your own badges (a list)
    Each one has (up to 20):
    • Badge name
    • Emoji
    • Earned for (Messages sent, Reaching a level, Coins (wallet and bank), Rep points, Daily quests done)
    • How many
    • Role to give (optional)

Commands

  • /achievements: See every badge and how close you (or someone else) are to each one.
